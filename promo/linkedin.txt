Forecast combinations improve the accuracy and reliability of forecasts by bringing together information across multiple models, components, and related series 📈🔗

Next month at #ISFconf2026 I'm instructing a new workshop on forecast combinations in decomposition, ensembles, and reconciliation. The workshop will feature a 'combination' of some probabilistic theory and lots of practical applications and exercises on real data in R with tsibble, fable, feasts, and distributional.

Key topics:
🧩 Decomposition forecasting: combining forecasts of trend, seasonality, and the rest.
🤖 Ensemble forecasting: averaging multiple models for better forecasts
🕸️ Forecast reconciliation: share information between related time series
📊 The distributional statistics behind probabilistic forecast combinations

It's one of eight fantastic workshops to choose at the 46th International Symposium on Forecasting by the International Institute of Forecasters: https://isf.forecasters.org/program/workshops/
